Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ethungahakotuwa Muslim Central College collects, uses, maintains and discloses information collected from users of this website. It applies to the Site and the products, services, features and resources offered through it.

1. Personal identification information

We may collect personal information when users visit the Site, submit an inquiry, register for an activity or use services available through the Site. Information may include a name, email address, telephone number and other relevant details voluntarily provided by parents, guardians, students, teachers or visitors. Users may browse anonymously. Choosing not to provide information may limit access to certain services.

2. Non-personal identification information

We may collect technical information such as browser name, device type, operating system and Internet service provider to understand usage and improve website performance and accessibility.

3. Cookies

The Site may use cookies to improve user experience and understand website usage. Users can configure their browsers to refuse cookies or notify them when cookies are sent; some website functions may be affected.

4. How we use collected information

Information may be used to improve the website and educational resources, respond to inquiries, provide school-related announcements and updates, and communicate with parents, guardians, students and other relevant parties about school activities and services.

5. How we protect your information

The College adopts reasonable data collection, storage, processing and security practices to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Secure communication technologies may be used where applicable. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Sharing personal information

The College does not sell, trade or rent users’ personal identification information. Information may be shared with authorized service providers where necessary to operate and improve the Site, or disclosed where required by law or lawful request. Shared information will be handled with appropriate care and used for legitimate purposes.

7. Third-party websites

The Site may link to third-party educational platforms, social media services, video services and external resources. The College does not control their content or privacy practices. Users should review the privacy policies and terms of those services.

8. Changes to this policy

The College may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated date will be revised when changes are published. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.

9. Acceptance

By accessing and using this website, users acknowledge that they have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Users who do not agree should discontinue use of the Site and avoid submitting personal information.
