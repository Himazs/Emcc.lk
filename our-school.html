THE EMCC STORY

Rooted in heritage. Growing with purpose.

Ethungahakotuwa Muslim Central College was established on 1 December 1949 and has served learners in the Kuliyapitiya area of Kurunegala District for more than seven decades.

Our learning journey spans primary, secondary and Advanced Level education. Our priorities are quality education, discipline, knowledge and values, with a continuing commitment to innovation, inclusivity and educational excellence.

Its story belongs to generations of students, families, educators and community members who have contributed to the life of the school.
